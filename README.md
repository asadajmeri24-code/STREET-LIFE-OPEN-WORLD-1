# LIFE STREET 🌆

**LIFE STREET** ek 3D Open World Android dream app hai jahan friends ek saath milte hain, ghoomte hain, social media use karte hain, aur games khelte hain.

## 👥 Main Characters (V1)
- **Asad** – Main Hero
- **Taiyaba** – Asad ki Girlfriend
- **Aman** – Best Friend
- **Faizan** – Best Friend
- **Talha** – Best Friend
- **Sahal** – Best Friend

## 📱 V1 Core Features
- **Friends Hangout:** Asad, Taiyaba, Aman, Faizan, Talha aur Sahal ke saath ek space/area mein milna aur ghoomna.
- **Working Phone (In-App Social App):**
  - Instagram jaisa interface
  - Reel banana aur share karna
  - Chatting (messages)
  - Calling (voice/call experience)
  - Aur other phone utilities
- **Working PS5 + Connected TV:**
  - TV ke saath connected PS5
  - TV par playable mini-game / game chalana

## 🔮 Future Expansions (V2+)
- 20 City Areas (Asad's Home, School, City Centre, Shopping Mall, Hospital, Police Station, Airport, Railway Station, Beach, Village, Car/Bike Showroom, Restaurant, Cinema, Stadium, Park, Business District, Luxury Area, Industrial Area, Benzo's Territory)
- School System (Class 8–10, Principal Office, Classrooms, Playground, Canteen, Library, Computer Lab)
- Clubs & Private Hangout Room
- 15 Villains (Main Boss: Benzo, plus Laila, Moni, Jumanji, Tomi aur baaki)
- Online Multiplayer Rooms, Real Voice Chat, Cars, Bikes, Money & Missions
